# Hi there 👋

I’m a **Data Scientist** focused on **statistical modeling** and **decision-oriented analytics**. I work across the data lifecycle — from data integration and exploratory analysis to modeling, visualization, and communication for technical and non-technical stakeholders.

I currently work at **CEPTRO / NIC.br**, contributing to analytical systems and studies related to **connectivity indicators** and **network quality** in Brazil.

## 🔍 What I Work On

**Applied Data Science & Analytics**  
Designing and delivering data-driven analyses using **R, Python, and SQL**, with a strong focus on statistical modeling, exploratory data analysis, and decision support on complex, real-world datasets.

**Reproducible Analytical Systems**  
Building and maintaining **reproducible analytical workflows**, reports, and dashboards that emphasize transparency, traceability, and long-term usability.

**End-to-End Project Ownership**  
Taking analytical projects from **problem formulation and data integration** through modeling, visualization, and communication of results.

**Interdisciplinary Communication & Collaboration**
